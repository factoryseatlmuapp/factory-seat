Factory Seat
A career mode for Le Mans Ultimate

Start as an LMGT3 rookie and race your way up through LMP3 and LMP2 to a Hypercar seat, one season at a time. You race in Le Mans Ultimate as usual; this app gives you each race weekend's briefing, reads your results when you finish, and runs the championship, your reputation and the team offers between seasons.

Good to know
- The app only reads Le Mans Ultimate's results files. It never changes your game files or settings.
- Your careers are saved in your Windows user folder (AppData\Roaming\LmuCareer), so reinstalling or updating keeps them.
- It's free. If someone charged you for it, that wasn't us.
