# Code of conduct

Factory Seat is a hobby project for people who love endurance racing. Keep this place friendly, like a good paddock.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to LMU, to GitHub, or to English.
- Give feedback on the app, not the person. "This screen is confusing" helps; insults don't.
- Assume good intent. Most rough-sounding messages are just lost in translation.

## Not OK

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Hate or discrimination of any kind, including about nationality, language, gender, sexuality, religion, or disability.
- Sharing someone's personal information without their permission.
- Spam, or advertising unrelated to Factory Seat.
- Posting cracked or pirated game content, or links to it.

## Reporting

Use **Report content** in the ··· menu on the comment, issue, or discussion. If it's about security, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What happens

The maintainers may edit, hide, or delete comments, lock threads, and block people from the repository, depending on how serious it is. Most of the time a friendly reminder is enough.

This applies everywhere Factory Seat lives on GitHub: issues, discussions, and pull requests.
